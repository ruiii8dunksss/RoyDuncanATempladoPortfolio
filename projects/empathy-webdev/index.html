Phantom Assassin

Mortred strikes swiftly from the shadows. Her Stifling Dagger and Blur allow her to hunt and escape, while Coup de Grace delivers devastating critical hits. The moment she finds her prey, Phantom Assassin strikes. Instantly closing in on a target, she effortlessly dodges attacks as she relentlessly cuts away at her foe. Without warning, any one of her attacks could prove suddenly and brutally fatal.
